# Future design research — Gamification & Player Identity

2026-11-26 — User-proposed long-term opportunity; deferred, not implementation authority.

Explore achievements, meaningful milestones, customizable player avatars or skins, and an evolving guitar-as-avatar that visually reflects a musician's journey. Consider guitar collections, instrument cosmetic upgrades and personalization of the Studio/Workbook environment. Preserve Sound Uncharted's Studio Atlas identity and professional, guitar-first experience.

Future research: review evidence on gamification in education and instrumental skill acquisition, intrinsic/extrinsic motivation, autonomy, competence, relatedness, novelty effects, retention and transfer. Compare achievement designs and optional cosmetic systems against a non-gamified baseline. Evaluate long-term voluntary engagement, learning outcomes, perceived pressure, accessibility, and commercial viability. Do not assume increased engagement proves learning or improved retention.

Design guardrails: existing Practice XP/Character Level are engagement measures, not demonstrated musical proficiency. Evidence-backed skill achievements must be distinguishable from participation and creative milestones. Cosmetics must not increase proficiency, alter evidence, restrict necessary learning resources or confer paid progression advantages. Favor optional personalization, transparent direct purchases and/or earned cosmetics over randomized rewards, loss-framed streaks, manipulative scarcity or pressure to overpractice. Any paid product or economy requires separate pricing, age-appropriate safeguards, consumer protection and accessibility review. Players should be able to minimize gamification.

Potential monetization hypotheses: optional guitar skins and visual customization packs, profile/studio themes and Workbook covers; premium creative tools remain a separate proposition. No store, rewards economy, avatar model, asset generation or payment integration authorized. Revisit after core visual prototype and research; no changes to DESIGN-001-D2 priorities, application code, database or production.
