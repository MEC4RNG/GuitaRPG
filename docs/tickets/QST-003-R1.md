  taxonomy, and writes the complete Quest graph atomically.

  persistence RPC with the accepted SES-001 `startPracticeSession` adapter.
- `/generate` now provides real Quick generation, structured preview, atomic

- No Custom builder, Result, History, progression, Training, recommendation, or
