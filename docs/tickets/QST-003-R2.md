# QST-003-R2 — Training Generation Mode Enablement

**Status:** IN PROGRESS

## Discovered defect

Canonical Quest semantics include `TRAINING`, but QST_GEN_V1 exposes only Quick and Custom generation and the authenticated generated-Quest persistence RPC rejects TRAINING. TRN-003 cannot truthfully persist a selected Training Quest until this boundary is enabled.

## Objective

Add a bounded `generateTrainingQuest` entrypoint that reuses Custom composition/finalization with `generation_mode = TRAINING`, then forward-replace the existing persistence RPC to accept exactly QUICK, CUSTOM, and TRAINING without weakening ownership, taxonomy, graph, or atomicity guarantees.

## Generator enablement

- `generateTrainingQuest(input)` requires an explicit canonical, generator-capable `primary_skill`.
- Training and Custom share the same template selection, composition validation, constraint assembly, canonical finalization, and DIF_V1 evaluation implementation.
- Identity origin remains `QST_GEN_V1`; metadata retains `QST_GEN_V1`, `QST_GEN_RULES_V1`, template ID, seed, and `TRAINING` mode.
- Every one of the 15 TRN_CAND_V1-capable Primary Skills produces a canonical persistence-ready Training Quest.
- Ranking, recommendation evidence, challenge preference, progression, and personal difficulty are absent.

The DORIAN Training fixture preserves the accepted demand profile: TECHNIQUE 56, FRETBOARD 48, THEORY 34, RHYTHM 52, CREATIVE N/A, TEMPO 45, CONSTRAINT 51, overall 54 / III.

## Persistence and security

Migration `20260929100000_qst_003_r2_training_mode.sql` forward-replaces only the existing RPC mode allowlist. QUICK, CUSTOM, and TRAINING are accepted; DAILY, CALIBRATION, and CAMPAIGN remain rejected. No schema or grant is broadened.

The existing boundary continues to derive ownership only from `auth.uid()`, require `QST_GEN_V1`, resolve active correct-kind taxonomy, persist the complete graph atomically, reject duplicate identity and invalid taxonomy, and expose no browser service credentials. A persisted Training Quest starts through ordinary SES-001 semantics and remains unchanged after Session start. Account deletion cascades the Quest and Session.
