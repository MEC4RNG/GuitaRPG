# TRN-002 — Recommendation Scoring and Ranking

**Status:** IN PROGRESS

## Objective

Define `TRN_SCORE_V1`, a deterministic and explainable heuristic priority ordering over a valid `TRN_CAND_V1` candidate set. Scores express relative modeled training-priority signals only; they are not probabilities, weakness percentages, mastery gaps, or predicted Quest success.

## Scoring model

The final `recommendation_priority_score` is clamped to `0.00–100.00` and is the sum of five independently visible components, each rounded to two decimals:

| Component | Maximum | Rule |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Intent Need | 30 | REFRESH 30; DEVELOPMENT 28; CALIBRATION 24; MAINTENANCE 10 |
| Proficiency Need | 20 | Rated Skills: `20 × (1 - proficiency/100) × (0.5 + 0.5 × confidence/100)`; UNRATED 0 |
| Evidence Need | 15 | UNRATED 15; ESTIMATED `15 × clamp((60-confidence)/60)`; ESTABLISHED 0 |
| Goal Alignment | 20 | exact Skill 20; otherwise Domain 10; otherwise 0 |
| Rotation Need | 15 | recency 0/3/7/10 plus exposure 5/4/2/0 at the accepted boundaries |

Every component includes its points, maximum, machine-readable explanation codes, and the factual inputs used. The complete `TRN_CAND_V1` candidate snapshot is retained, so the total is independently recomputable. READY_V1 remains a preparedness classification; bounded recency answers the separate question of whether a Skill has received recent attention.

## Ranking and ties

`SCORE_DESCENDING_DENSE_RANK` assigns equal scores equal semantic ranks and preserves TRN_CAND_V1 order only for deterministic serialization inside a tie. A unique top key is emitted only for one true maximum; otherwise it is null and every tied key is retained. All eligible candidates are returned.

For a healthy new Player, all 15 candidates score `54.00`, rank 1, and form a 15-way top tie. An exact Skill goal raises its candidate to `74.00`; a Domain goal raises each capable candidate in that Domain to `64.00`.

## Goal and context semantics

Structured Skill and Domain matches are the only goal inputs. Skill and Domain matches do not stack. Raw `player_goals.priority` is preserved as evidence but deliberately has no weighting semantics; free-text objectives remain unparsed and score zero.

Challenge preference, typical session duration, and default tuning are carried through unchanged but do not affect Skill priority. Challenge preference belongs to later concrete Quest composition. XP, Character Level, practice time, Attributes, and personal difficulty are not inputs.

## Accepted fixtures

- DORIAN Hybrid Picking: DEVELOPMENT, proficiency `55.30`, confidence `20`, recent exposure `1` → `47.36`.
- DORIAN Scale Mapping and Syncopation Control: exposure-only UNRATED, recent exposure `1` → `43.00` each.
- Stale established fixture: proficiency `55`, confidence `80`, LOW readiness, exposure `6`, over 30 days → `48.10`.
