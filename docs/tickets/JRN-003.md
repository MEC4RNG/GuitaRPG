## JRN-003-R1 — SVG alignment and CI remediation

User authorized focused remediation of the initial musical-block browser and formatting failures.

- Earlier browser checks failed six per-engine copies of a legacy chord alignment test because it measured an inline glyph that the SVG renderer intentionally hides. The original test could no longer assess the visible marker.
- Replaced the stale glyph measurement with browser geometry of the rendered SVG circle (via `getScreenCTM` and `DOMPoint`), compared against the matching semantic fret-cell center in **both** axes, with strict subpixel tolerance.
- That instrumented check exposed a genuine **2px horizontal mismatch** at mobile viewport widths in Chromium, small-phone Chromium, and mobile WebKit (desktop Chromium, Firefox, and WebKit passed). The SVG was sized from a container narrower than the fret rows' intrinsic minimum width.
- Refined the shared chord presentation to keep the SVG, six-string control strip, and fret rows at the same intrinsic width within one horizontally scrollable, bounded region. This avoids scaling a diagram independently of its clickable cells and preserves the no-page-level-overflow contract.
- Corrected the Prettier line-wrap violation in `components/workbook-editor-prototype.tsx` and removed a temporary CI formatting diagnostic, leaving `package.json` unchanged.
- Existing V1 document structure, Player ownership, Supabase trusted RPCs, historical revisions, production deployment, and the JRN-004 authorization boundary remain unchanged.
- **Gate:** passing final-head production, security, UX browser matrix, persistence and accessible navigation; real user visual review before JRN-003 merge. Do not claim success while final-head checks remain pending.
