# TRN-004 — Adaptive Quest Composition & Challenge Preference

**Status:** COMPLETE

## Objective

Define `TRN_ADAPT_V1`, `TRN_CHALLENGE_TARGET_V1`, and `TRN_VARIANTS_V1` so a Player-selected Skill remains fixed while a bounded deterministic set of canonical TRAINING Quest variants is evaluated by DIF_V1 and DIF_PERSONAL_V1 and matched to the Player's challenge preference.

## Challenge-preference contract

`TRN_CHALLENGE_TARGET_V1` defines ordered personal-difficulty bands and primary-gap anchors:

| Preference | Personal Level order | Gap anchor |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| RELAXED | II, I, III, IV, V | -20 |
| BALANCED | III, II, IV, I, V | 0 |
| CHALLENGE | IV, III, II, V, I | 25 |
| PUSH_ME | IV, V, III, II, I | 35 |

PUSH_ME prefers Stretch before Overreach. These labels are not proficiency and never influence TRN_CAND_V1 or TRN_SCORE_V1.

## Deterministic variant catalog

`TRN_VARIANTS_V1` generates LOW, BASE, HIGH, and MAX recipes for each current family while holding Primary Skill, Quest Type, and selected Concept constant:

- Technique: bounded string-set, tempo, and MAX fret-range ladder
- Rhythm: 60 / 80 / 120 / 180 BPM
- Fretboard: bounded fret-range and string-set ladder
- Theory: 240 / 120 / 60 / 30 second response windows
- Ear: 6 / 10 / 16 / 24 prompts
- Creative: 2 / 4 / 8 / 16 bars

Tier names are search keys only. Every variant is canonically generated and evaluated with actual DIF_V1 and DIF_PERSONAL_V1 output. Tests prove nondecreasing absolute demand for all six families and all 15 capable Primary Skills remain materializable.

## Selection and fit

For rated Primary Skills, selection first minimizes the Player's preference-order index, then distance to the preference gap anchor, then uncertainty (LOW, MODERATE, HIGH), then stable LOW/BASE/HIGH/MAX order. No Skill substitution occurs.

Fit is one of:

- `EXACT_PREFERRED_BAND`
- `CLOSEST_AVAILABLE_BAND`
- `PRIMARY_UNRATED_CALIBRATION_FALLBACK`

An UNRATED Primary always selects BASE, remains personal difficulty UNKNOWN, and does not apply challenge preference. Rated fixtures are nondecreasing in personal challenge from RELAXED through PUSH_ME.

## Context novelty limitation

Only Contexts actually present in the Quest receive deterministic keys. Default tuning produces `tuning:<slug>` with UNKNOWN novelty from `EXPLICIT_SNAPSHOT`; preference is not evidence of familiarity. A Quest without relevant Context uses LOW / `NO_RELEVANT_CONTEXT` with no keys and no modifier. No generalized Context table or inferred exposure is introduced.

## Provenance and separation

`metadata.training_recommendation` remains the complete TRN_MAT_V1 explanation of why the Skill was selected. `metadata.training_adaptation` records model/catalog/policy versions, challenge preference, whether it was applied, fit, selected variant, target order/anchor, selected personal evaluation, Context novelty, and a compact four-variant evaluation summary.

The Quest remains QST_GEN_V1 / TRAINING with its absolute DIF_V1 profile unchanged. Personal difficulty is separate, retains status and uncertainty, and has no XP, proficiency, confidence, readiness, or Attribute mutation authority. Typical session duration remains deferred.

## Application behavior

The Training preview shows preference, absolute demand, personal challenge, evaluation status, uncertainty, fit explanation, and the Context-familiarity limitation. New Players see a factual calibration fallback rather than a fabricated personal Level. Persistence, durable retry, Session start, regeneration, and recommendation provenance continue through the accepted TRN-003 flow.
